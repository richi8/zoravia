- **Date:** 2026-11-14
- **Status:** done
- **Depends on:** C33 (and the Region 1 chunks C28–C31 it mirrors)

## Summary
Region 2, **Emberwaste**, ships as its own pack, `packs/emberwaste`. It has:
- a 200×200 volcanic desert with three towns and 11 zones;
- 24 new Zorlings, two Titans and the legendary Calderon;
- 11 route trainers, a trial and badge, Rival Mira, and a region boss;
- a full campaign with four side quests;
- its own start entry.

Ash storms set every battle in three zones Burning. Ember Pass now links Verdale and Emberwaste both ways. The user chose to do the whole region in one chunk (not split like C28–C31), the name Emberwaste, and Fire / Earth / Nature starters. In one preview round they approved the map and my look picks. Four small, general engine changes were needed (see Deviations). The playthrough simulator finishes the region at Verdale's pace.

## Decisions
- **Decision:** An expansion attaches its region by writing the link on its own end; links work from either end (`linkedArrival`).
  - **Why:** "add a region with zero engine changes" should also mean zero changes to core's map. Without the pack loaded, Ember Pass simply leads nowhere.
  - **Alternatives considered:** setting `to` on Verdale's connection, which makes core name a pack it doesn't depend on, or an override of the 550 kB core map.
- **Decision:** Ash storms are a per-zone `battleWeather` list, separate from cosmetic `weather`.
  - **Why:** DESIGN (C32) says overworld weather never changes battles. A storm only in some zones makes it a place to plan around, not a region-wide tax. Story battles take the weather of their NPC's zone.
  - **Alternatives considered:** every battle in the region Burning. That is harsh in the starter zones, and it would make Water trivial everywhere.
- **Decision:** Starters are Fire / Earth / Nature (user's pick): Fire > Nature > Earth > Fire, with two of the region's types.
- **Decision:** Looks were picked at a preview round: the user took my picks (Saguardian 3423, Pyrebeetle 3435, Voltsting 3493, Calderon 3524, Brambleroll 3473, Dustwhirl kept as the dark bat).
- **Decision:** Calderon is Heatproof and breaks on **Frozen** (soak, then freeze), not Steam Burst.
  - **Why:** In the caldera's storm, Calderon on Steam Burst made any Water hit both the reaction and ×2.25 damage. Brute force won 98% (`bosssim` scratch runs). With Frozen the plan matters: reactions 35% / 63% / 80% at +0 / +2 / +4, brute force 8% / 10% / 33%.
  - The aura is ×1.75 at Lv 35, because at ×2 the plan team won only 42%.
- **Decision:** Gorran mirrors Vex (36 / 37 / 38 with the badge, ×1.5), and his test checks the hint instead of "not trivial at his level".
  - **Why:** a team with the hint (Ice + Earth) wins 85% at his level. Teams without it win 0–5%, and about 40% at +2. That is the C31 rule (the hint must matter). The Verdale-style "< 50% at −1" check fails only because that test's team *is* the hint team.
- **Decision:** Mira's Slagwatch ace is the **middle stage** at Lv 26 (30 with the badge), not the final form.
  - **Why:** a final form below its Lv 32 evolution walled the counter-picked plan (up to 9 tries). Verdale's Infernyx exception still stands there.
- **Decision:** The `instead` cap is raised from 4 to 8.
  - **Why:** a travelling rival counters two starters per region she knows. Mira has 5 here, and region 3 will add 2.

## Deviations from plan / design
- **Engine changes (each small and general; the plan allows them if logged):**
  1. **Map travel.** New `src/game/world/map/links.ts` (`linkedArrival`, `connectionAt`). `OverworldOptions.onTravel`: stepping onto a linked connection saves the new place and hands it to the host. The title scene and debug launcher replace the scene with the next map's, with a 0.4 s fade. Needed for any region to be reachable from another (C23/C28 follow-up).
  2. **Zone `battleWeather`** (`src/game/data/maps.ts`, refs checked). The overworld passes it into every battle started in the zone. `simulateBoss` and the playthrough simulator take weather too. Needed for DESIGN's "Ash storms: battles start with Burning".
  3. **`instead` cap 4 → 8** (`src/game/data/trainers.ts`).
  4. **Shipped pack list** (`packs/index.ts`), loaded by `main.ts` and the balance CLI.
- `DESIGN.md` updated:
  - Links from either end, travel and respawn per map.
  - Emberwaste's summary (storms, roster, bosses, campaign, travelling Mira).
  - Per-zone battle weather.
  - The start list.
- The region took one chunk instead of four (user's choice), so the map, roster, campaign and balance passes are each thinner than Verdale's C28–C31. The roster still got a look preview round, as the memory asks.

## Issues & known limitations
- **Battle backdrop is the C10 forest placeholder for every biome**, so desert battles show green hills (seen in the storm-battle screenshot). A desert backdrop is new art and should go through a preview round first.
- The respawn center is per map. After walking back to Verdale, the player wakes at Verdale's first center until they use one there.
- Travel replaces the overworld scene, so wild Zorlings and NPC wandering restart on each crossing. There is no "Ember Pass" sign or toast on arrival yet.
- The Dunewyrm Titan's brute-force margin is thin at +2 (reactions 100% vs brute 73%). That is the storm's nature: it makes the reaction free, which is the region's lesson.
- The title background is still the first start region's map (Verdale) even when continuing an Emberwaste save (C33 follow-up).
- `map.json` adds about 0.5 MB to the bundle (lazy-loading maps is still a C37 item).
- Overlay positions for later NPCs were fixed by hand after tests caught them: Odo stood in the shop footprint, Flint on a lamp, Vell on the well.
