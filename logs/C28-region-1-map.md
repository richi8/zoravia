## Summary
Region 1 now has a name, **Verdale**, and a 200×200 hand-laid map in `packs/core/maps/verdale/`. It has three towns with real buildings (Willowmere hub, Fernhollow, Galecrest outpost) and 10 roads. The Silverrun river and a marsh stream are crossed by 52 generated bridges. There are 11 zones in T1–T3 with spawn tables, two Titan lairs, the legendary's Heartwood Shrine, the Crown Rock boss arena, three landmarks, three secrets and two exits toward regions 2 and 3. The world tool gained an "authored layout" mode. The game gained building props (style A, picked at a preview round) whose doors open the center and shop services. Every walkable tile is reachable from the start, which is tested. "Play: Verdale" starts a new game there.

## What was built
- **Building art**, `src/game/world/buildings.ts`: Zorling center (5 tiles wide, red roof, gold-ringed heart emblem), shop (blue roof, awning, potion sign), houses (4 roof colors, door left or right), well, sign, fence, lamp, menhir (standing stone with a rune), and bridge decks for both directions with rails on the open sides. They use fixed `TOWN_RAMPS` instead of biome ramps, so a house looks the same in every region. Storybook style "A": tinted outlines, dithered shading, shingle roofs, timber posts.
- **Props**, `src/game/world/props.ts`: the 10 new kinds plus `PropInfo` fields:
  - `footprint`: buildings block their whole footprint. The prop's tile is the door in the bottom row; `propFootprint()` handles the mirrored houses.
  - `flat`: bridge decks never hide characters (depth 0).
  - `walkway`: a deck opens its tile over water.
  - `service`: `center` / `shop`.
  - `Painter` paints biome or town materials (`PaintMaterial`). `terrain.ts` centers building sprites on their footprint.
- **Collision and doors**, `src/game/world/map/world-map.ts`: footprints and walkways in `refreshCollision`, and `doorAt(x, y)`. `OverworldSim.facing()` treats a center or shop door as the nearest NPC with that role (within 6 tiles), so `E` at the door opens the service. The minimap draws buildings in their roof color and bridges as path.
- **World tool, authored layout**, `tools/world/layout.ts` + `layoutSchema` in `config.ts`:
  - Layout inputs: towns, routes (wobbly path polylines), rivers, lakes, islands, ponds, clearings (anchors; optional tree ring with a gap), fords (walkable lily pads), zone seeds (nearest seed with a noise warp, plus `trees`/`grass`/`wet` density shifts), and exits.
  - Bridges are placed wherever a route crosses water.
  - `connectPockets` opens unreachable walkable pockets by removing the props in the way, and blocks what stays cut off.
  - The old ring generator still runs when there is no `layout`. Test-region output is unchanged.
- **Verdale**, `packs/core/maps/verdale/`:

## Decisions
- **Decision:** An authored layout mode in the generator instead of hand-painting 200×200 with overlay rects.
  - **Why:** "Hand-curated" here means placing the shapes that matter (towns, roads, lakes, zones, sites) by hand. The noise detail between them is what a generator does well. Overlay rect edits for a map this size would be thousands of lines and hard to change.
  - **Alternatives considered:** the C19 ring generator plus a big overlay. The rings put T2 sites in T3 on a non-centered hub and can't place a river or a lair. A click-to-paint editor (C19 follow-up) is still worth doing, but it's a tool of its own.
- **Decision:** Zones come from authored seeds (nearest seed, warped by noise), not from rings.
  - **Why:** lairs and the shrine must sit in specific tiers. Seeds still give concentric tiers, which is tested by mean distance from the hub.
- **Decision:** Buildings are props with a footprint, and the door tile is the prop's tile.
  - **Why:** placement, baking, depth sorting (walking behind a roof) and streaming all work unchanged. A separate "structures" layer would duplicate all of it.
- **Decision:** No interiors. The attendant stands beside the door and `E` on the door talks to them (user approved at the preview round).
- **Decision:** Unreachable pockets are opened by clearing props, and blocked only when water cuts them off.
  - **Why:** this guarantees "whole region walkable" without hand-fixing hundreds of tree-enclosed tiles. Blocking the rest keeps wild Zorlings from spawning where the player can never go.
- **Decision:** Region 1 lives in the `core` pack, and the test region stays alongside it.
  - **Why:** C29 replaces core's test species with Region 1's, so Region 1 is core content. Regions 2 and 3 come as their own packs (DESIGN roadmap). The test region still carries the C21–C27 debug content (trial, rival, shrine, sample quest) until C30 moves the story into Verdale.

## Issues & known limitations
- **Bundle size:** `map.json` is 547 kB raw / 63 kB gzipped and is bundled in the pack module. Vite's chunk warning now shows a 1.1 MB shared chunk. Lazy-loading maps is still open (C19 follow-up).
- The overlay uses straight rects, so town streets are tidier than the wobbly roads outside, and lanes to house doors are about 2 tiles wide.
- The in-game map (`M`) shows 200 tiles at 1 game pixel per tile at 480×270. It is readable, but small.
- Spawn tables reuse the 10 core test species at T2/T3 levels (a wild Sproutle at Lv 16–22). C29 replaces them.
- The Grove Trial, Rival Mira and the Glacivar shrine still live on the test region. Verdale only has their anchors (`trial`, `legendary`, the `titan-*` lairs, `boss-crown-rock`).
- The secrets are reachable places with nothing in them yet. Hidden items need a pickup mechanic (C30).
- Signs can't be read yet.
- Clicking a building door does not walk there and talk. Click-to-walk only targets NPCs.
